    Use without internet
    1. While online, save this page to your phone with your browser's Save page or Download option.
    2. Open the saved file any time. It needs no signal.
    3. Contacts you add stay in that browser on that phone. Different browsers keep separate lists.
    4. If contacts disappear after you close the browser, it is not allowing storage. Write down your key numbers.

    Distances are straight-line, not road distance. Phone numbers you add are stored only on this device.
